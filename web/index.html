    How it works

    Sensors→ESP32→Raspberry Pi 5→Isolation Forest→Dashboard

    The ESP32 reads seven sensors once a second and sends them to the Raspberry Pi. The Pi keeps the last 5 readings, turns them into 12 features and scores how far they are from normal running.
